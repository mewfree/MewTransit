* North star
Personal Montreal transit app. Open the map, see where you are, tap a nearby stop, know what comes next.

Agencies in scope: *STM* (metro + bus), *REM*, *Exo* (trains first, buses later).

Two repos:
- this one — Expo app
- [[file:../mewtransit-api/readme.org][mewtransit-api]] — Cloudflare Worker that owns GTFS and serves the app

Old local SQLite (~assets/SQLite/exo_trains.db~) is gone. The 2023
~transit-worker~ experiment (raw STM dump into D1) is retired — STM is too
big to ship as a dump.

** Data notes
STM static: ~https://www.stm.info/sites/default/files/gtfs/gtfs_stm.zip~
(page says current file valid until 2026-10-25).
Exo trains: ~https://exo.quebec/xdata/trains/google_transit.zip~
Exo also publishes a pile of bus-sector zips — later.
REM: first-party URL still TBD; Transitland archives the feed.

STM ~stop_times~ is huge. Ingest must normalize (agencies, routes, stops,
trips, calendar, stop_times) with indexes on ~(stop_id, departure_time)~.
Never ship the 1.4GB dump again.

Live data is a first-class goal, not a nice-to-have. Static GTFS is the
fallback; GTFS-RT trip updates should drive the minutes on the map.
